Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of how Sky Jump handles information while you play.

Last updated: 28 October 2026

At a glance: Sky Jump is designed as an offline, single-player arcade game. Gameplay records such as your best score or height may be stored locally on your device and are not sent to the developer.

1 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Sky Jump handles information when you play the game.

Sky Jump is designed as an offline, single-player arcade game. This policy applies to the Sky Jump mobile application.

2 Information We Collect

Sky Jump is designed not to collect, request, or transmit personal information to the developer.

The game may store gameplay information, such as your highest score or best height, locally on your device to preserve your progress between sessions.

This locally stored gameplay information is not sent to the developer.

3 Data Sharing

The game does not share personal information with the developer or third parties, provided the published version contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or other third-party data-collection services.

4 Internet and Permissions

Sky Jump is intended to run offline without internet access or unnecessary device permissions.

The permissions and components included in the final application determine its actual behaviour.

5 Data Storage and Deletion

Saved gameplay records may remain on your device until they are reset by the game, if supported, or the application's data is cleared or the application is uninstalled.

The developer does not maintain a server containing your gameplay records.

6 Children's Privacy

Sky Jump does not intentionally request personal information from players. If the game targets children, the developer must also comply with applicable children's privacy laws and Google Play's Families policies, where relevant.

7 Third-Party Services

This policy assumes that the published version does not include advertising networks, analytics tools, tracking SDKs, or other third-party services that collect user data. If any are introduced, this policy must be revised to describe them accurately.

8 Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the game's features or information practices change. Any update will include a revised date.
